# Flashcards – L12

Topic: [[L12 Stability II]]

Phase margin?::PM = 180° + ∠βA(ωgx), where |βA(ωgx)| = 1.
Gain margin?::GM = −20log|βA(ωpx)|, where ∠βA(ωpx) = −180°.
Two-pole loop with ωgx exactly at ωp2 (ωp1 ≪ ωp2): PM?::45° (Razavi Ex 10.4): −90° from ωp1, −45° from ωp2.
Closed-loop peaking at ωgx for PM = 45° and 60°?::45° → 1.3/β (30% peak); 60° → exactly 1/β.
Allen’s rule of thumb for “stable enough” from a step response?::Fewer than about three rings: PM of at least 45°, preferably 60°. PM is a small-signal guide; big steps also slew (Razavi).
Why is ≈ 60° phase margin the usual target?::No frequency peaking, little overshoot, fast settling; more margin is slower, less rings.
