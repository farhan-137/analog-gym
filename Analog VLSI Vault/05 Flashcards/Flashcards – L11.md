# Flashcards – L11

Topic: [[L11 Stability I]]

Barkhausen’s criteria?::|βA(jω1)| = 1 and ∠βA(jω1) = −180°: the loop returns the signal in phase and at full size.
How do you find the loop gain of a real circuit (Razavi)?::Set the input to zero, break the loop, inject a test voltage Vt and measure what returns, VF: βA = −VF/Vt.
Why is a one-pole feedback amplifier always stable?::One pole adds at most 90° of lag; the loop phase never reaches −180°.
Gain crossover vs phase crossover?::ωgx: |βA| = 1. ωpx: ∠βA = −180°. Stable when ωgx comes well before ωpx.
Does a higher closed-loop gain make a feedback amplifier more or less stable?::More stable: smaller β lowers |βA|, moving ωgx to lower frequency; the phase plot does not change.
