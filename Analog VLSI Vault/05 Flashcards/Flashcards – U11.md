# Flashcards – U11

Topic: [[U11 Five-transistor OTA]]

Gain of a five-transistor OTA?::Av = gm1(rO2 ‖ rO4).
Why is Gm of the 5-T OTA equal to gm1 and not gm1/2?::The mirror copies M1’s signal current into the output, where it adds to M2’s: 2·(gm·vd/2) = gm·vd.
5-T OTA input CM range?::Vov5 + VGS1 ≤ Vin,CM ≤ VDD − |VGS3| + Vthn.
5-T OTA output range?::Vov5 + Vov2 ≤ Vout ≤ VDD − |Vov4|.
