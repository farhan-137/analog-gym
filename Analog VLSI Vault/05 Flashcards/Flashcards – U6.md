# Flashcards – U6

Topic: [[U6 Impedance rules, sources, diodes, mirrors]]

Resistance looking into a gate?::∞ (no DC gate current).
Resistance into a drain with RS under the source?::rO + (1 + gm·rO)·RS ≈ gm·rO·RS.
Resistance into a source with RD on the drain?::(RD + rO)/(1 + gm·rO) ≈ 1/gm + RD/(gm·rO).
Current-mirror output current?::Iout = IREF·(W/L)out/(W/L)ref (λ = 0).
Small-signal resistance of a diode-connected MOSFET?::1/gm ‖ rO ≈ 1/gm.
Headroom cost of a diode-connected device?::A full |VGS| = |Vth| + |Vov|, not just |Vov|.
