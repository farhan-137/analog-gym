# Flashcards – L1

Topic: [[L1 Performance parameters]]

Closed-loop gain of a feedback amplifier?::A/(1 + βA) ≈ 1/β.
Gain error in terms of β and A?::ε = 1/(1 + βA) ≈ 1/(βA).
Minimum open-loop gain for error ε at closed-loop gain Acl?::A ≥ Acl/ε (e.g. 10/0.01 = 1000).
Unity-gain frequency of a one-pole op amp?::ωu = A0·ω0 (gain × bandwidth).
Closed-loop bandwidth?::(1 + βA0)·ω0 ≈ β·ωu.
Output error caused by input offset Vos at closed-loop gain 1/β?::Vos/β (the offset is amplified by the closed-loop gain).
