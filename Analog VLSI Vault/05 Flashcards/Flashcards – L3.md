# Flashcards – L3

Topic: [[L3 Design procedure]]

The six design steps (Ex 9.7)?::Power → currents; swing → headroom; choose overdrives; W/L; check gain (lengthen off-path devices); bias voltages at the edges.
How do you raise gm·rO without changing Vov?::Scale W and L up together: W/L (and Vov, gm) stay, λ ∝ 1/L drops, rO rises. gm·rO ∝ √(WL/ID).
Linear scaling by α: what changes?::gm ×α, rO ÷α, power ×α, CL capability ×α. Vov, gain and swing do not change.
