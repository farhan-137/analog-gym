# Flashcards – U10

Topic: [[U10 Differential pair]]

Split Vin1, Vin2 into CM and DM parts.::VCM = (Vin1 + Vin2)/2; vd = Vin1 − Vin2.
At what vd is the tail fully steered?::√2·Vov, where Vov is the overdrive at balance (ID = ISS/2).
Why is the tail node AC ground for a differential signal?::One side’s current rises exactly as much as the other’s falls, so the tail current and VP do not change.
CM gain of a resistor-loaded pair with tail RSS?::ACM = −RD/(1/gm + 2RSS): each half sees 2RSS.
Lowest input CM of an NMOS pair?::VSS + VISS + VGS1 (tail headroom plus a full VGS).
Highest input CM of a resistor-loaded NMOS pair?::VD1 + Vth (Q1’s fence).
